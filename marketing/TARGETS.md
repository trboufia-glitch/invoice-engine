## Rules that protect the domain

- **Send one at a time, from `pontboufia@gmail.com`.** Ten in a row from a fresh
  inbox is the pattern spam filters key on. Space them across a morning.
- **Never send twice to the same address.** If `hello@` bounces, try the listed
  alternative once — then stop.
- **Do not attach anything.** No PDFs, no spreadsheets.
- **If a firm replies asking what it costs, that is the sale.** The browser tool
  is free forever; the API is what you would sell them.
- **No follow-up.** The copy says "I won't follow up", so it must stay true.
  That restraint is what makes the email credible.
